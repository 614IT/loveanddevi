NATASHA DEVI 
**Hi beautiful friends. My name is Natasha, born in Canada, ancestry India by way of Guyana. 
I was introduced to kambo in 2017. I read a little about this medicinal secretion before partaking in receiving the medicine. I was very intrigued to learn kambo enhances intuition and fight or flight response, in addition to giving a full body cleanse. I was even more amazed to learn its main function was to repair DNA because it is full of bioactive peptides, I came to this conclusion after realizing kambo is targeting different areas of the body via bioactive peptides, the same thing our DNA is comprised of, thus repairing. 
 I HAD TO TRY THIS. 
Upon receiving the medicine for the first time, in the Amazon of Peru, I felt its effects seconds after application. Heat rising in my body, something scanning me up and down from the inside, a tingling in my hands and feet, heart racing ... and then I had a beautiful vision. I remember walking in the jungle, lots of flowers and green around me, a waterfall up ahead and I followed two people as they walked toward the waterfall... my deceased father and grandmother. I felt as if I had been walking with them for hours. I had passed out. When I came to, the people around me told me I had passed out and was only gone for seconds. Water over my head, multiple times, I was back. I would later find out I had passed out four times by this remarkable medicine, my body was shaking a bit, I was moving like a snake with my eyes rolled back. Amazing that three little dots of frog secretion could do this to my body. (Later I would research and find the physical symptoms were that similar to someone experiencing a kundalini awakening.) 
Prior to kambo, at my retreat, the ayahuasca was not having a real affect on me. After kambo, the ayahuasca activated in full swing. 
I felt so connected to this medicine. The frog came to visit me in an Ayahuasca ceremony. 
After returning to the states, kambo was still in my mind. I shared my story with many. I continued researching this medicine. It was calling me. 
I returned to Peru a few months later, with my mother, whom had a strong desire to connect with kambo after I shared my knowledge of this medicine. 
That same year I began an apprenticeship with kambo. Though I am here in jungle, working directly with source of medicine, I am still learning 5 years later. 
I have shared medicine in Peru, Mexico, USA, Holland, Germany and Belgium. I have hosted trainings, retreats, and workshops, as well as personal and group ceremonies. 
This medicine is something of magic. The spirit of the frog is strong when it is running through your body. How lovely it is that this medicine, although a poison to its predators, is medicine to us, his allies. 
There is an amazing symbiotic relationship between the kambo frog and humans. I am proud, enthusiastic, and grateful to share this medicine with all who are seeking. 
Peace. Love and Kambo.**
