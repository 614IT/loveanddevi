The lodge is located in primary jungle of the Pacaya Samira National Reserve. Nearly 5 hours from the city of Iquitos. 
 
Birds surround the property and life is abundantly living. Hammocks and rocking chairs and an area to swim right at the front of our property. 
The lodge is equipped with 4 modern toilets and 4 showers for our guests. 
 
There are 4 tambos on the property. A tambo is the name used for sleeping quarters. Each tambo can accommodate 4 guests and allows privacy with aid of partitioning. There is a bed for each guest accompanied by mosquito net. 
 
There is no internet or electricity, though generator is used once a day to allow guests to charge their electronics.
