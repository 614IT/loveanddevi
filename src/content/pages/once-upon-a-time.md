**Natasha. Devi. Sahadeo. 
 
lover of love, truth, nature, and music.** 
 
**I was born in Canada, raised in Florida, and briefly lived in New York. I am now living in the Amazonian jungle of Peru. 🌿** 
**My family is from India by way of Guyana, the only predominantly English speaking country in South America, so I am learning Spanish as I journey. 
I was working as an attorney in New York but felt completely unfulfilled. I fell into a kind of depression from being in that concrete jungle.. also a bit of heartache was surrounding me. 
I have lost many family members and friends at young ages... people who were working hard to build their foundation so they could begin to travel the world. What pushed me to begin my journey was the death of a childhood friend, younger than me, who was killed violently in Florida by someone she should have been able to trust. 
Wait, no more. 
All I have is now. 
All there is, is NOW. 
My depression was lingering and I knew I needed to do something. I knew New York was not the place for me. 
I sold all my things, quit my job.. and traveled to the jungle for healing.. to heal with sacred medicines.. to meet Mother Ayahuasca. 
She helped me remember all the love I ever need is inside of me, I was born with it.. she reminded me I had been disconnected and I needed to reconnect to Mother Earth. 
It was so healing, I brought my mother to have her own journey a few months later. 
I began a blog of my plant medicine journeys (which I am still updating from diary entries) 
I moved to Peru and am now learning from plant medicines and shamans here; I organize retreats for all those wanting their own healing.. wanting to connect back to Mother Earth. 
All we need is already within us. 
I love to travel, but have found the most fulfilling journey I've embarked on, was and is, the journey within.** 
 
** 
Peru is a beautiful place, the jungle is even more majestic. I fell in love with the nature, tranquil living and healthy foods. 
I have a strong passion for Kambo medicine, continuously training and learning and am now a practitioner for the healing gifts this magical creature has given us. 
I have much more to learn from the Amazon. There are so many wondrous healing plants and animals that I wish to know, learn, and share. 
 
Mother Earth is truly amazing.** 
 
**I feel a strong purpose to help people connect back to Mother Earth- through nature, the medicinal plants and animals ... and to not forget about the spirit world.** 
**nature, the wise, and my intuition are my teachers. 
 
love is my religion. 
 
i am still learning. 
 
i am constantly evolving, as are each of you. 
 
peace. love and devi.**
