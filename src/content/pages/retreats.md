Love and Devi is based in the Amazon Jungle and sis working alongside different indigenous medicine people to bring a truly authentic experience for those feeling called. 
Fly to Iquitos, Peru and connect back to Mother Earth as we venture into the Amazon. Depending of the activities you wish to partake in while here in the jungle will determine the location of the Amazon we will travel to.

The retreats can encompass many activities, such as:

- Jungle exploration
- Observation and swimming with pink and grey dolphins
- Observation of 13 species of monkeys in their natural state
- Participation in medicinal plant ceremonies with native shipibo shamans
- Hiking in primary jungle (low and high jungle)
- Explanation of medicinal plants
- Observation of sloths in their natural state (perezosos)
- Night jungle walk to search for tarantulas, snakes, scorpions, frogs, toads, jaguars, insects and much more
- Observation of giant lily pads (water lily Victoria Regia)
- Camping or lodge accommodation (flexible)
- Visits to native villages / local communities
- Search for kambo frog to excrete medicine
- Working one on one with indigneous medicine people
- And much more...

All the activities are flexible according to your personal interests. 
 
All food is included in the price as well as transportation to and from the lodge (located in the jungle) and Iquitos, Peru. 
(\*\*Flights to Peru and accommodation in Iquitos are not included.)
